Our Story

PageTurner was founded by a team of lifelong learners who couldn't find a single destination for high-quality, practical digital books. So we built one.

Our Story

From Frustration to Revolution

In 2020, our founders were tired of paying premium prices for outdated, padded-out books that delivered little real value. They believed readers deserved better — curated, expertly-written, immediately actionable digital books at a fair price.

PageTurner launched with just 12 books. Within 18 months, we had grown to 500+ titles across 8 categories, serving readers in over 90 countries. Every book in our catalog is hand-vetted by our editorial team for quality, accuracy, and relevance.

Today, PageTurner is the destination of choice for ambitious professionals, entrepreneurs, developers, and investors who are serious about leveling up.
